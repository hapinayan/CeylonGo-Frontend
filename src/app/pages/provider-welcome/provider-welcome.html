How It Works

Get your tourism business listed and verified in 4 transparent, automated steps.

Step 1

Email OTP Verification

Enter your official email and verify instantly with a secure 6-digit one-time password.

Step 2

Submit Details & Drafts

Fill in personal, business, and district address details with autosave at every step.

Step 3

Upload KYC Documents

Upload clear photos of your National Identity Card (NIC) and a verified portrait selfie.

Step 4

Admin Review & Activation

Fast 24-hour review by CeylonGo administrators, email approval, and password setup.
