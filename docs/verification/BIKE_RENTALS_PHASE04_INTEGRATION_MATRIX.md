## Remaining requirements and external acceptance

- Backend and Product need to align quote, booking, and deposit amounts. A quote is a price estimate from the server, while booking and payment responses are authoritative for the actual reservation and charge. Live responses for booking and payment status remain unverified.
- Confirm T-06 publication and hub association policy. Align visibility on public child endpoints with public detail or document their intended exposure. The frontend only calls the audited read paths after public detail succeeds.
- Confirm T-03 catalogue identifiers and mappings, stable pagination (single multi-type query and deterministic tie-breaker), and any expanded filtered rating/popularity or subtype contract. These are current backend gaps, independent of the UI's preview controls.
- Keep TR-05 policy gates open for T-14 cancellation and refunds, A-04 business evidence, X-01/X-11 licence and permits, X-06 inspection evidence/storage, X-10 seating, T-18 reminders, and X-07 late-return rate snapshots.
- External acceptance needs authorized isolated SQL Server with migrations and concurrency/ledger evidence, approved PayHere sandbox and refund path, and authenticated Angular/Flutter staging with approved users and document access. See TR-05 external handoff and staging runbook.
