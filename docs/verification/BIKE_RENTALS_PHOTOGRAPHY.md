# Bike Rentals Photography

The built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool produced these illustrative assets for this work. No third-party stock photograph was downloaded or licensed. The project should review generated imagery under its applicable OpenAI and brand terms before external publication. The photos do not depict real CeylonGo fleet vehicles, properties, providers or availability.

| Destination | Source and processing |
| --- | --- |
| `public/images/bike-rentals/coastal-road.webp` | Generated travel editorial scene, resized to 1,680 pixels wide and encoded as WebP. Used as a decorative hero backdrop. |
| `src/development-assets/rental-preview/` | Six distinct panels cropped from one generated contact sheet and encoded as 508 × 320 WebP images. Blue scooter, black motorcycle, mountain bicycle, red scooter, white motorcycle and electric bicycle. The development target alone copies these assets. |

## Generation prompts

- Hero. “Photorealistic premium Sri Lanka travel editorial website hero, ultrawide landscape composition. Beautiful curving coastal asphalt road bordered by Indian ocean and coconut palms, bright tropical sun, a single helmeted motorcycle rider riding away on the RIGHT third, unobstructed scenic ocean and deep-blue shaded negative space on left for website heading. Realistic, no lettering, no logos, no watermark.”
- Contact sheet. “A 3 column by 2 row perfectly aligned edge-to-edge contact sheet, six distinct premium photoreal landscape rental catalog photos in Sri Lanka, no gutters, no labels or text. Each panel independently framed with entire vehicle centered and generous margins. Top row left blue modern step-through scooter by coastal road, center black naked motorcycle in Galle with sea, right mountain bicycle with palms. Bottom row left red scooter in tropical hillside, center white sporty motorcycle by coastal buildings, right electric bicycle on misty Ella road. Exact equal panel divisions. Each vehicle is an illustrative unbranded example.”

Live listing images come from the listing API. Card and gallery images use descriptive alternative text, lazy loading and a visible fallback on missing or broken photos.
